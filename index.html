Understanding Market Participants

01 / FOREIGN INSTITUTIONAL INVESTORS

FIIs represent foreign hedge funds, sovereign wealth capital, and global asset managers. They hold massive capital reserves and are the primary trend drivers in the Indian equity and derivative markets.

02 / DOMESTIC INSTITUTIONAL INVESTORS

DIIs comprise Indian mutual funds, insurance companies (like LIC), and domestic pension funds. They serve as long-term market anchors and provide strong structural liquidity support.

03 / RETAIL & PRO CLIENTS

Retail traders encompass individual non-institutional traders. Historically, retail participants are heavily net-long on call options during market tops and net-short during market bottoms.
